# INDIA SURVEY TEK — 3D Geodetic Surveying & Civil Engineering Website (`v1.0.0`)

Official company portfolio and interactive engineering workspace for **INDIA SURVEY TEK**, a Government-Approved Surveying, Engineering Survey, and Mapping Consulting firm headquartered in **Krishnarajapuram, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India**.

---

## Key Features (`v1.0.0`)

- **Interactive 3D Surveying Background (`Three.js`):** Real-time 3D topographical elevation mesh, rotating Total Station with sweeping laser beam, floating RTK survey prisms, LiDAR point cloud, and switchable daylight field photography backdrops.
- **Civil Engineering & Land Survey Calculators Suite:**
  1. **Revenue Land Area & Gunta Converter:** Instant conversion across Acres, Karnataka Guntas (`1/40 Acre`), Sq.Ft, Sq.Meters, Hectares, and `30×40` sites.
  2. **Civil Earthwork Cut & Fill Estimator:** Calculates excavation and embankment volumes in `m³`, `CFT`, and `Brass` from Existing Ground Level (`EGL`) vs. Formation Level (`FGL`).
  3. **Slope & Road Gradient Calculator:** Computes slope percentage, `1 in N` gradient ratio, and inclination angle.
- **Undertaken Services Showcase:** Topographical Survey, Contour Maps, Quantities Survey, Settingout Work, Layout Designs & Marking, Township/Estate Surveys, and Registration/Khatha Sketches & Poodi.
- **Filterable Project Portfolio & Case Study Modals:** Interactive filtering across *Government*, *Private*, and *Mapping Consulting* projects.
- **Leadership & Team Profiles:** Highlighting Founder & Principal Surveyor **K T Shadakshari**, Senior Survey Specialist **Muruli K P**, and Survey & Sketch Engineer **Devaraju K**.
- **Direct Contact & Google Maps Integration:** Embedded interactive map pinned to **Diesel Loco Shed Rd, Duravani Nagar, Krishnarajapuram, Bengaluru 560036**.
